about=
    AI Agent by TerraLab is an AI agent for QGIS. Describe what you need in a prompt: it loads the data, runs the analysis, styles the map and builds the layout, in the project you already have open, and shows each step as it goes.

    • Free tier with no card. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. Plans and limits: https://terra-lab.ai/pricing

    • 226 tools: load and inspect data, style and label maps, run Processing algorithms, edit geometry, build print layouts

    • 192 open data connectors: OpenStreetMap, Copernicus, NASA, USGS, IGN, Overture, Planetary Computer and 185 more

    • A catalog of 9,901 ready datasets, from national sources in 194 countries and from EU-wide and global ones, each with its licence

    • You stay in control: you see every step before it runs, changes wait for your approval, and one click undoes anything it changed

    • Fits your habits: one conversation per project, and attach files, layers, selections or the map view to a message

    • Benchmarks: on Google Research's GeoKTools and GeoExpertQueries it scored 43.8 out of 100 on 41 held-out public questions at Low effort, against 24.9 for Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the nkarasiak QGIS MCP server in Claude Code and 22.1 for the jjsantos01 QGIS MCP server in Codex, graded by Google's own scoring code. It also passed 62.8 percent of 160 GeoBenchX tasks and scored 61.1 out of 100 on GS-QA2. Method and every score: https://terra-lab.ai/blog/ai-agent-qgis-plugin-geospatial-benchmark

    • QGIS 3.28 to QGIS 4, on Windows, macOS and Linux

    • 18 languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Indonesian, Japanese, Chinese (simplified and traditional), Vietnamese, Arabic, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Korean

    • Answers come from an AI model. It can be wrong; review what it changes

    • Sends your request and a description of the open project to agent.terra-lab.ai, in the European Union. Your layers and files stay on your machine. See https://terra-lab.ai/privacy-policy

    • Guide: https://terra-lab.ai/blog/ai-agent-complete-guide

    • Other TerraLab plugins, which AI Agent can run for you once installed: AI Segmentation extracts buildings, trees and other objects as vector polygons (https://plugins.qgis.org/plugins/AI_Segmentation/). AI Edit turns an orthophoto into a site plan or a project visual (https://plugins.qgis.org/plugins/AI_Edit/)

    By TerraLab, terra-lab.ai
