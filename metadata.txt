about=
    AI Agent by TerraLab is an AI agent for QGIS. It works on your own layers, in the project you already have open, and shows each step as it goes.

    • Benchmarks: on Google Research's GeoKTools and GeoExpertQueries it scored 43.8 out of 100 on 41 held-out public questions at Low effort, against 24.9 for Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the nkarasiak QGIS MCP server in Claude Code and 22.1 for the jjsantos01 QGIS MCP server in Codex, graded by Google's own scoring code. It also passed 62.8 percent of 160 GeoBenchX tasks and scored 61.1 out of 100 on GS-QA2. Method and every score: https://terra-lab.ai/blog/ai-agent-qgis-plugin-geospatial-benchmark

    • 227 tools: load and inspect data, style and label maps, run Processing algorithms, edit geometry, build print layouts

    • 89 open data connectors: OpenStreetMap, Copernicus, NASA, USGS, IGN, Overture, Planetary Computer and 82 more

    • A catalog of 2,830 ready datasets, from national sources in 184 countries and from EU-wide and global ones, each with its licence

    • You stay in control: you see every step before it runs, changes wait for your approval, and one click undoes anything it changed

    • Fits your habits: one conversation per project, and attach files, layers, selections or the map view to a message

    • Free tier with no card. Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. Plans and limits: https://terra-lab.ai/pricing

    • QGIS 3.28 to QGIS 4, on Windows, macOS and Linux

    • Works with AI Edit and AI Segmentation when those TerraLab plugins are installed

    • 12 languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Indonesian, Japanese, Chinese (simplified and traditional)

    • Answers come from an AI model. It can be wrong; review what it changes

    • Sends your request and a description of the open project to agent.terra-lab.ai, in the European Union. Your layers and files stay on your machine. See https://terra-lab.ai/privacy-policy
